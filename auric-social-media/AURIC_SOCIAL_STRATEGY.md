## 1. The idea everything hangs on

> **Many disciplines. One core.**

AURIC's mark is a sun: **12 rays around one solid core.** The campaign reads it literally:

- **The rays are the disciplines:** strategy, content, design, motion, video, voiceover, websites, apps, systems, AI, VR and project management.
- **The core is the one team, and the one person, who holds them together.**

That gives the account a visual system that comes from the logo, not one placed on top of it:

- **Explaining the offer:** the sun becomes a diagram (Post 03).
- **Showing progress:** the sun lights up ray by ray (Post 09). This is the same idea AURIC's Workspace product uses.
- **Closing a post:** the sun becomes a quiet watermark (Post 12).

> **Confirm with the founder:** whether the 12 rays were designed to mean disciplines. The campaign presents it as *"how we
> work"*, not *"what our logo means"*, so it is honest either way.

## 2. Voice

| Is | Isn't |
|---|---|
| Plain and specific: "A website that books appointments." | Abstract: "Digital solutions for tomorrow." |
| Honest about fit: "If it doesn't, we'll say so." | Sales-y: "The best agency in Egypt." |
| Short sentences, then a turn: "Projects rarely break at the idea. They break in between." | Slogans with nothing behind them |
| "We" = the team, "you" = the client's business | Corporate third person |

Banned phrases: next level, unleash, elevate, cutting-edge, seamless, one-stop shop, solutions (as a noun on its own),
game-changer, "in today's fast-paced world".

## 4. Art direction: the 12 formats

Consistency comes from shared tokens: type, colour, margins, labels and the full stop. Variety comes from the composition.
No two formats share a layout.

| # | Format | Purpose | Composition | Type | Image | Structure | Link to the website |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Brand statement** | Declare a belief | One mega headline (3–4 lines), copy below, mono footer | Unbounded 800, 100–170px, gold full stop | None, or the glow and grid | Single | Hero and BigCTA sections |
| 2 | **Educational carousel** | Teach one thing in 4–6 steps | Giant outlined numeral, question, answer; alternate dark and cream | Numeral 300px outlined; h2 92px | Calendar or diagram on the last slide | Cover → steps → summary/save | How we work numerals, Strategy calendar |
| 3 | **Service showcase** | One capability | Cover: capability name + its pill; then real work slides | Name 200px+ | Real work in frames or full-bleed | Cover → 3–4 proof → list → CTA | Division headings with the gold full stop (Services page) |
| 4 | **Problem / solution** | Meet a pain in the client's words | Big quote headline at top, gold-ruled answer at the bottom | h2 94px in curly quotes | None | Cover with chat bubbles → problems → CTA | Plain voice; chips |
| 5 | **Technology & innovation** | AI, automation, VR | Perspective grid or a system diagram | Mega + outlined phrase | Geometric, built from the system | Single or short carousel | Grid texture, code pill |
| 6 | **Creative conceptual** | A memorable single idea | One visual metaphor built from brand elements (the sun as a diagram) | Mega headline, small labels | Sun, rays, leader lines | Single | The logo itself |
| 7 | **Portfolio / case study** | Prove it | Full-bleed still or framed screen; title, one-sentence brief, deliverables in mono | h2 66–76px | Real only, labelled | Cover → context → 3–5 visuals → result → link | Case study pages, `.slot` labels |
| 8 | **Behind the scenes** | Show the making | Timeline, storyboard or edit screenshots in frames | Mono labels dominate | Real process captures | Carousel or Reel | Timeline pill, frames |
| 9 | **Brand storytelling** | Why AURIC exists | Narrative across 5–7 slides, broken with cream "beat" slides | Mix of mega and body | Minimal | Story arc → turn → CTA | Services intro, "One partner" |
| 10 | **Team & culture** | The people | Portrait, name, role, one real sentence from them | h3 name, mono role | Real photos only | Single or carousel | Team page |
| 11 | **Business insights** | Advise owners | Cream canvas, claim, 2–3 reasons, a takeaway | h2 + rows | None | 4–6 slides | Hairline rows |
| 12 | **Campaign announcement** | Launch or offer | Gold canvas, short headline, contact rows | Mega, `#141414` | Sun watermark | Single + Story | BigCTA |

## 5. The launch campaign: "One partner."

*"One partner."* is the website's own Services headline. The 12 posts introduce the evolved AURIC in four acts of three posts each.
Each act gets a week, publishing Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday (see the [calendar](AURIC_CONTENT_CALENDAR.md)).

| Act | Posts | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **I. The idea** | 01 Introducing · 02 The gap · 03 Twelve rays, one core | Who AURIC is, the problem it exists to fix, the integrated model in one image |
| **II. The capabilities** | 04 Build · 05 Create · 06 Strategy | Proof first: real screens and real films, then teaching |
| **III. The rest of the sun** | 07 Experience · 08 Manage · 09 One project, start to finish | The newest pillar, the glue (management), then all of it working together |
| **IV. Working together** | 10 Sound familiar? · 11 How we work · 12 Let's make it real | The client's problems, the process, the invitation |

**Why not one post per pillar in order:**

- Five service ads in a row would turn the account into the thing the brief says to avoid.
- So the capabilities are spread out and given different jobs:
  - Build and Create are **proof**.
  - Strategy is **teaching**.
  - Experience is a **concept**.
  - Manage is a **single strong statement**.
- Post 09 then shows them **connected**, which is the actual positioning.

**Feed rhythm** (the newest row is on top):

| 09 · dark | 08 · gold | 07 · dark |
| 06 · dark | 05 · cream | 04 · dark |

### Before publishing (blocking)

1. **Post 07 (Experience):** confirm AURIC delivers VR and interactive work. If not, publish the backup in its place: *"Built in two languages"*, a Knowledge post on bilingual products using the real Mizan Arabic/English screens.
2. **Post 03:** confirm the founder is comfortable with the 12-rays reading of the mark.
3. **Post 04:** confirm Mizan's owner is happy for the Arabic dashboard to appear on social. The screens contain only synthetic demo data (README), and the project is already public on the site.
4. **Post 05:** the TCG, ELK Café, Digitmask and Green Teaching Kitchen work is already published on the site as client work. Confirm the social use is covered by the same permission.
5. **Arabic:** the slides are in English. Have a native copywriter produce the Arabic caption versions (draft notes are in each `caption.md`). Optionally render Arabic slide versions, using Alexandria and IBM Plex Sans Arabic, which are already in `assets/fonts/`.

## 6. After the launch: the steady-state mix (per month)

Twelve posts a month, 3 per week, plus Stories 4–5 days a week.

| Per month | Pillar |
|---|---|
| 3 | Work & proof (one case study carousel, one Reel cut from real footage, one single) |
| 3 | Knowledge (save-worthy carousels) |
| 2 | Capabilities |
| 2 | Brand / perspective |
| 1 | Conversion (packages, a call invitation) |
| 1 | Culture, once team photography exists; until then, another Knowledge post |

**Reels:** cut from material that already exists in `public/media/`:

- the showreel (vertical cut exists)
- TCG reel
- event recap
- 15-second motion versions of Posts 01, 03 and 09, following the motion notes

**LinkedIn:** same posts and order.

- Carousels upload as PDF documents (`export/carousel.pdf`).
- Singles use the 4:5 image, or the 1200 × 627 version where provided.
- The captions are longer, with more context and fewer hashtags (3 at most).

## 7. Measuring it

Set a baseline in week 0 from Instagram and LinkedIn insights. **No targets are invented here.**

| Question | Metric | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Is the new positioning landing? | Saves + shares per post; comments that repeat the idea ("one partner") | Insights |
| Is Knowledge useful? | Save rate on Posts 06, 10 and 11 vs. the others | Insights |
| Is proof convincing? | Profile visits and link taps after Posts 04, 05 and 09 | Insights, website analytics (`@vercel/analytics` is installed) |
| Is it creating business? | DMs and WhatsApp chats that mention a post; contact-form submissions in campaign weeks | Inbox, EmailJS log |

**Review after week 4:**

- Keep the formats with the best save and share rates.
- Retire the weakest one.
- Plan the next 12.
