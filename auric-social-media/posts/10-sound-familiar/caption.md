# 10 · Sound familiar?

## A. Content concept

| | |
|---|---|
| Objective | Meet the audience's real problems in their own words, and turn recognition into a conversation. |
| Audience | Prospects who know something is wrong but haven't named it. |
| Core message | Four common problems, and what AURIC would actually do about each. |
| Pillar | 06 Conversion / 02 Knowledge |

## B. Copy

**Headline:** Sound familiar?

**Supporting copy:** "Our website doesn't look like us anymore." "We post every week. Nobody gets what we actually do." "The whole business runs on Excel and WhatsApp." "We have four freelancers and nobody in charge."

**Caption:**

```
Four situations we hear about from business owners, and what we'd do about each:

1. "Our website doesn't look like us anymore." → Rebuild it around the brand you have today, easy for your team to update.
2. "We post every week. Nobody gets what we do." → A content plan with three clear messages and real work as proof.
3. "The business runs on Excel and WhatsApp." → A system built around your workflow, with the repetitive parts automated.
4. "Four freelancers. Nobody in charge." → One team, one timeline, one owner.

Which one is yours? Reply with the number.
```

**CTA:** Reply with the number; we'll tell you honestly what it needs.

**Hashtags (optional, post them in the first comment):** #SmallBusiness #Marketing #WebDesign #Automation #Egypt

**Arabic note:** مألوف؟ (draft; a native writer should rephrase the four problems in natural Egyptian and Gulf Arabic)

## C. Design direction

- **Note:** the quotes are paraphrased situations, not testimonials. They carry no names or attribution.
- **Cover (cream):** "Sound familiar?" with four chat bubbles alternating ink and gold.
- **Slides 2–5:** alternate ink and cream. The problem appears as a large quote (h2 94px, gold curly quotes hanging in the margin). A gold rule and "What we'd do" with a two-line answer sit at the bottom, and the top label names the pillars involved ("Build + Create").
- **Slide 6 (gold):** "Which one is yours?" and a dark button.

## D. Execution

- Carousel, 6 slides at 1080 × 1350. LinkedIn: `export/carousel.pdf`.
- **Story:** a poll with the four problems.
