# 02 · The gap — Projects rarely break at the idea.

## A. Content concept

| | |
|---|---|
| Objective | Name the problem AURIC exists to solve, so the positioning reads as a solution rather than a service list. |
| Audience | Owners and managers who have run a launch with several freelancers or agencies. |
| Core message | Projects fail in the handoffs between separate providers. AURIC closes the gaps with one team and one owner. |
| Pillar | 01 Brand & identity / 05 Perspective |

## B. Copy

**Headline:** Projects rarely break at the idea. They break in between.

**Supporting copy:** One project. Five people. Every handoff loses something. And you become the project manager.

**Caption:**

```
Most projects don't fail because the idea was bad.

They fail between the designer and the developer. Between the video editor and the marketer. Between the brief you wrote and the version that reached the fourth person.

Every handoff loses a little: the tone, the deadline, the reason you started. And the person holding it all together ends up being you, on top of running the business.

That gap is why AURIC is built the way it is: strategy, creative, technology and delivery in one team, with one owner for your project.

If your project is split across too many people, tell us about it.
```

**CTA:** Start a project, or reply with the slide that felt familiar.

## C. Design direction

- **Slide 1 (cream):** mega headline, with "They break in between." in gold-deep. Below it, a chain of five outlined pills (Design, Web, Video, Ads, You) joined by broken dashed lines. "You" is the gold one: the person stuck in the middle.
- **Slide 2 (ink):** "One project. Five people." with numbered hairline rows; mono notes on the right ("the look", "the website", … "usually you").
- **Slide 3 (bg):** "Every handoff loses something." Four rows fade from 100% to 30% opacity: the brief, the tone, the deadline, the reason it started.
- **Slide 4 (cream):** "You become the project manager." Big type, short copy.
- **Slide 5 (ink + glow):** the sun mark beside "One team. One owner.", with chips for the five pillars and Manage highlighted.
- **Slide 6 (gold):** "One partner." (the website's Services headline) and a dark button.
- **Labels:** Geist Mono, with an "01 / 06" counter top right.
