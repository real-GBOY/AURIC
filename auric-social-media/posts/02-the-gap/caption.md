# 02 · The gap — Projects rarely break at the idea.

## A. Content concept

| | |
|---|---|
| Objective | Name the problem AURIC exists to solve, so the positioning reads as a solution rather than a service list. |
| Audience | Owners and managers who have run a launch with several freelancers or agencies. |
| Core message | Projects fail in the handoffs between separate providers. AURIC closes the gaps with one team and one owner. |
| Pillar | 01 Brand & identity / 05 Perspective |

## B. Copy

**Headline:** Mind the gap. / We close it.

**Supporting copy:** none

**Caption:** (final version, with hashtags, alt text and LinkedIn copy: `social-copy.md`)

```
Five people, five briefs, one launch. Guess who ends up holding it all together?

The designer waits on the copy.
The developer waits on the designer.
The video editor is on version seven.
And you're the one connecting all of them, on top of running the business.

Every handoff loses something: the tone, the deadline, sometimes the reason you started.

Swipe to see how we close that gap: strategy, creative, technology and delivery in one team, with one owner.

Send this to someone who's managing too many freelancers right now.
```

**CTA:** Share: Send this to someone managing too many freelancers

**Hashtags:** #ProjectManagement #SmallBusinessEgypt #MarketingAgency #Freelancers #DigitalPartner

## C. Design direction

- **Slide 1:** five tall blocks (Design, Website, Video, Ads, You), tilted and scattered, with gold dashed cracks between them. "You" is the gold block. Headline: "Mind the gap."
- **Slide 2:** the same blocks snapped flush into one solid bar, with the AURIC sun above it. Headline: "We close it."
- Before and after in two swipes, with no copy needed.

## D. Execution

- Carousel, 2 slides. **Motion (Reel, 5 s):** the scattered blocks slide together and lock with a click, then the sun rises above them.
