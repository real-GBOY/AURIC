Question one02 / 06
01

Who is it for?

"Everyone" is not an audience. Pick one clear group, and every decision after it gets easier: the words, the look, the channel.

Question two03 / 06
02

What should they do next?

Call, book, buy, visit, remember. Give every piece one job. A post that tries to do four things usually does none.

Question three04 / 06
03

Where will they meet it?

A reel, a website, a billboard and a sales deck are four different conversations. Same message, made for each place.

Question four05 / 06
04

How will we know it worked?

Choose the number before launch, not after: enquiries, bookings, sign-ups, sales. Then look at it every month.

That's strategy.

A plan that gives every piece a job, before anyone opens a design file.
