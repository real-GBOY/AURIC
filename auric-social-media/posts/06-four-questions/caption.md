# 06 · Four questions before we make anything.

## A. Content concept

| | |
|---|---|
| Objective | Give real, save-worthy value, and show how AURIC thinks: strategy comes first. |
| Audience | Founders and marketers about to brief a designer, agency or freelancer. |
| Core message | Strategy means giving every piece of work one job before anyone opens a design file. |
| Pillar | 02 Knowledge & insights |

## B. Copy

**Headline:** Aim first.

**Supporting copy:** Then create.

**Caption:**

```
Before we design a logo, cut a video or write a line of code, we ask four questions:

1. Who is it for? One clear audience, not "everyone".
2. What should they do next? Call, book, buy, visit. One job per piece.
3. Where will they meet it? A reel and a sales deck are different conversations.
4. How will we know it worked? Pick the number before launch.

Answer these four and most creative arguments disappear. Save this for your next brief.
```

## C. Design direction

- **Concept:** strategy as a dartboard. Ink and cream rings with a gold bullseye, and a dart flying in from the headline. The four questions move into the caption.
- **Canvas:** cream, with the board cropped off the bottom-right corner so the poster feels large.

## D. Execution

- Single. **Motion (Reel, 4 s):** the dart flies in and hits the bullseye; the board shakes once.
