## B. Copy

**Headline:** One project, start to finish.

**Supporting copy:** Illustrative example: a new clinic. Strategy → identity → website and booking → launch content → one timeline, one owner.

**Caption:**

```
What does "one partner" actually look like? Here's an illustrative example: a new clinic opening.

1. Strategy: who the patients are, what makes the clinic different, what launch should achieve.
2. Identity: logo, colours, signage and templates, designed from that strategy.
3. Build: a website that books appointments, connected to the front desk.
4. Create: a launch film, reels and the first month of posts, all pointing at the booking page.
5. One timeline and one owner the whole way.

Watch the sun on each slide: it lights up as the project moves forward.

(An example for illustration, not a client case study.)
```

## C. Design direction

- **Concept:** the sun as a progress meter, the same idea as the AURIC Workspace. No rays are lit on the cover, then 2, 5, 8 and 11, then all 12 and the core on delivery. Unlit rays are charcoal (sand on cream).
- **Labelled clearly:** "Illustrative example · a new clinic" on the cover and in the caption.
- **Slides:**
  - cover (ink + glow, sun at 0)
  - strategy (bg)
  - identity (cream)
  - build (bg)
  - create (bg)
  - launch (gold, sun fully lit in #141414)
  - CTA (ink + grid, full gold sun, "What would your sun look like?")
- **Each step slide:** a progress block (sun plus a mono status such as "8 / 12"), an h2 headline, the copy, and chips for the disciplines involved.
